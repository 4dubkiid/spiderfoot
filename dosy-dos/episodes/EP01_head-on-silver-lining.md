## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Shooting Order (Recommended)
1. **Shoot wide (B-CAM/GoPro) first** — mount it, let it run. You can use it as safety and for full-body coverage.
2. **Then shoot A-CAM angles** — close-ups, slow-mo details, reaction shots.
3. **Capture B-roll in the same session** — road, hands on wheel, phone screen, neck rub. These are 5-second shots, easy to grab.

### Slow-Mo Shots to Prioritize (iPhone 15 Pro Max)
- Keys or phone falling in slow-mo (crash representation)
- Hand coming up to neck
- Smile breaking during the phone call reveal
- Anything with natural motion — leaves, traffic passing, light through a window

### Natural Color / Time-of-Day Strategy
| Time | Mood | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Golden Hour (1hr before sunset) | Warm, nostalgic, safe | ACT III — silver lining resolution |
| Midday / Overcast | Cool, flat, exposed | ACT I — crash aftermath, clinical feel |
| Late afternoon, directional light | Dramatic shadows | ACT II — the call, building tension |

### Graphics Needs
- Crash date title card: *"July 17, 2021"*
- Stat card: *"60 mph. Head-on. Both cars totaled."*
- Reaction card: *"What are the odds."*
- End slate: Dosy Dos branding

### Sound Design Cues
- **0:00 – 0:12:** Silence, then sudden impact SFX → hard cut to black
- **0:12 – 0:45:** Low ambient road noise under voiceover
- **0:45 – 1:45:** Neutral, slightly tense underscore
- **1:45 – 2:00:** Silence on the reveal beat — let it breathe
- **2:00 – 3:00:** Orchestral/piano swell, warm, rising to end slate
